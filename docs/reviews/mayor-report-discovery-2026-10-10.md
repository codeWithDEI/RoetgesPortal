# Mayor's report discovery — 10 October 2026

## Scope and conclusion

The project instructions now require reading public mayor's reports, including
public attachments, independently of the monitor's exclusions. Eleven existing
topics receive report-derived updates or additional evidence; four new topics
cover water-main works, a proposed Calisthenics/boules project, and proposed
cooling for the childcare centre and community hall. Every new report-derived
claim explicitly identifies the mayor's report in German. This is a local
editorial change for review, not independent approval or a deployment.

The public ALLRIS calendars for January through October 2026 were inspected,
limited to meetings on or before 10 October of the Rötgesbüttel council and its
registered committees, and the Samtgemeinderat. This yielded 16 meetings: eleven
municipal and five joint-municipality meetings. Their public mayor's-report and
`Anfragen und Mitteilungen` result pages were opened and read. Non-public
agenda details were not followed. This is not a complete audit of all historical
sources, all Samtgemeinde committee reports, or all 49 published topics.

The available report attachments for the Samtgemeinderat on 13 January,
14 April, and 23 June were downloaded through the public source page, read in
full, and visually inspected. Their document references are not a durable
archive. The January attachment is actually dated 8 October 2025; it is not
assigned a January 2026 activity date. The April document has a public heading
and public listing but an inconsistent `N.Ö.` footer; no new topic claim is
based on it. April and June mainly list subjects of earlier committee reports,
not their substantive outcomes. Such headings do not establish procurement,
construction completion, or resolutions.

All report sources added here were actually accessed on 10 October. Historical
sources retain their earlier access dates, except the flood-protection
resolution, which was reopened to verify the added decision metadata. Report
meeting dates are identified in source titles; no document publication date is
invented. Existing resolutions, stable topic IDs, locations, and earlier
milestones remain traceable.
