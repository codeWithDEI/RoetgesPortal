# Editorial source audit — 17 September 2026

## Scope and conclusion

Reviewed all **42 published topics** against their **79 distinct cited public ALLRIS pages**, including the available resolution text, discussion, votes, proposal history, summaries, descriptions, status explanations, decision outcomes, and milestones. Six additional result endpoints were checked; one supplied a substantive committee resolution and five returned “Keine Information verfügbar”. The published sports-facilities statute was additionally checked in the district official gazette.

Baseline: merged `main` commit `36e3af098d6a58daf2422eba15fd42065d732b3a`. The reviewed worktree at `fb77ca1cc9415549df46e73e4e8848658cbe4d61` has the same tree. The findings below describe that baseline, not the corrected files. The initial review changed no published topic, generated data, or production service. The subsequent corrections are recorded in the final section of this report.

**Recommendation: make an editorial correction PR before promoting the portal.** The previous status corrections were useful but incomplete. Most quoted amounts and descriptions of proposals are faithful. The remaining problems are predominantly transitions from a proposal or instruction into an asserted decision, implementation, or completed event.

The findings are deliberately distinguished:

- **Correction/evidence required: 18 topics.** This includes one directly contradicted committee outcome and other claims that exceed the cited evidence. It does **not** mean that all 18 real-world projects are known to have a different status.
- **Refresh required: 6 topics.** Past dates are still described as upcoming. A passed date does not prove that an event occurred or a proposal passed.
- **Traceability/wording improvement: 6 topics.** The substantive core is supported, but an individual link or negative assertion should be narrowed.
- **No material discrepancy found in the checked claims: 12 topics.** This is not certification that no later, unlinked decision exists.

This was a review of the cited public evidence, not a complete new search of every agenda, mayoral report, attachment, or non-public decision from 2025–2026. Attachments were not exhaustively audited. Absence of a public result must remain an explicit uncertainty. Geometric accuracy of map coordinates was outside scope.

## Findings to address before promotion

### 1. [P1] A committee recommendation is incorrectly reported as deferred

File: [community-hall-school-meals-compensation.yaml](../../content/topics/community-hall-school-meals-compensation.yaml), especially `latestDecision` lines 14–21, description, and the second milestone.

The portal attributes a deferral to the Schulausschuss on 2 June 2026. The actual public [committee resolution, TOP 8](https://www.papenteich.sitzung-online.de/public/to020?TOLFDNR=1006846) instead records unanimous adoption of the amended recommendation: include EUR 3,000 annually from 2027 and prepare an amendment to the agreement. The proposal's procedure identifies the committee's role as **Beratung und Beschlussempfehlung**, with the final decision assigned to the Samtgemeindeausschuss.

Correct the decision to an adopted **committee recommendation**, cite the result page, and remove the invented deferral milestone. Keep the final agreement explicitly unconfirmed. Neither “the agreement is finally adopted” nor “the committee deferred it” follows from this evidence. The broad `open` phase need not be changed solely because the committee recommended the measure.

### 2. [P1] A school IT recommendation is presented as an applicable standard

File: [school-it-standard-2026.yaml](../../content/topics/school-it-standard-2026.yaml), lines 3–7 and 39–42.

The [24 February committee result](https://www.papenteich.sitzung-online.de/public/to020?TOLFDNR=1004336&SILFDNR=1000096) supports the committee vote. However, the [proposal's procedure](https://www.papenteich.sitzung-online.de/public/vo020?VOLFDNR=1000636&refresh=false&TOLFDNR=1004336) assigns the final decision to the Samtgemeindeausschuss. That decision is not publicly documented by the listed sources. Its technical “Erledigt” marker does not establish adoption.

“Seit 2026 gilt …” and `implementation` are therefore not adequately supported. Until a final decision or implementation report is obtained, describe a recommended equipment standard, retain the proposed equipment and funding information as such, and use a phase such as `committee`. Do not replace this with `active` merely because the proposal mentions 2026.

### 3. [P1] Implementation is repeatedly inferred from a preparatory decision

The existing [editorial policy](../governance/editorial-policy.md) requires evidence that execution, procurement, or a commissioned review **has started**. Fourteen of the sixteen topics currently labelled `implementation` do not establish that transition in their cited evidence. This is an evidence-quality finding, not proof that nothing happened outside the published records.

Two particularly clear subject mismatches:

- [Sports-centre modernisation](../../content/topics/sports-centre-modernisation.yaml): the [17 March resolution](https://www.papenteich.sitzung-online.de/public/to020?TOLFDNR=1005886&SILFDNR=1000260) takes note of the VfL application, requests a 2026 assessment, and schedules consideration for the 2027 budget. It does not approve or document the start of the renovation. Keep the broad renovation issue `open`, or explicitly scope a separate item to the approved preparatory assessment. Do not mark the renovation itself `decided` as a substitute.
- [Central childcare responsibility](../../content/topics/central-childcare-responsibility-review.yaml): the [18 June 2025 resolution](https://www.papenteich.sitzung-online.de/public/to020?TOLFDNR=38466&SILFDNR=2127) instructs the municipality to **request a review from the Samtgemeinde**. It does not demonstrate that the Samtgemeinde accepted the request or started the review. `statusBasis` currently skips that distinction. Record the local resolution separately and leave the wider review unconfirmed.

Other affected topics are B4 side facilities, KiTa sanitary renovation, the old fire-station community centre, Digitalpakt planning, the Rötgesbüttel MTW, the fire-station platform, the three traffic-order/sign matters, the Pfänderweg crossing, the youth-welfare agreement, and the school IT standard. Do not downgrade every topic mechanically to `decided`: a local application does not mean that the competent authority approved the requested traffic restriction, and an assessment of renovation needs is not approval of the renovation.

## Why the existing pipeline does not prevent these errors

There is no protocol-to-topic fact extraction in the build:

1. `tools/scan_sitzung_online.py` discovers public agenda candidates and maintains an unpublished editorial queue. The current workflow produces review artifacts; it does not independently substantiate the final narrative.
2. Editors write `content/topics/*.yaml`, including `status`, `latestDecision`, descriptions and milestone outcomes.
3. `tools/build_portal.py` publishes those values. For example, `make_list_item()` copies the YAML status directly. It does not read minutes or infer approval.
4. `validate_topic_status_references()` verifies that the evidence URL belongs to the topic's source list. It does not prove that the linked document supports the claim, is a resolution rather than a proposal, or comes from the final decision-making body.

Therefore a passing build is not an editorial correctness signal. The observed source-to-claim mistakes originate in the editorial YAML and its evidence interpretation; repeatedly rebuilding will reproduce them faithfully.
