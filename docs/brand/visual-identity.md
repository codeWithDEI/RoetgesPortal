# Visual identity

RötgesPortal uses an independent word and image mark. Folded pages or a map
become the letter R, while a gold location marker establishes the local context.
The mark represents structured information about Rötgesbüttel without using an
official municipal symbol.

## Assets

- `web/public/roetgesportal-mark.svg`: compact image mark for the header, icons,
  and small formats
- `web/public/roetgesportal-logo.svg`: horizontal word mark with the claim
  “Unabhängig informiert”

The SVG files are the source assets. They use a transparent background, flat
colors, and no effects so they remain legible at small sizes and suitable for
print or plotting workflows.

## Palette

- primary petrol: `#006080`
- bright petrol: `#007DA4`
- deep petrol: `#00455C`
- location gold: `#E4B72E`
- neutral text: `#444444`
- white surface: `#FFFFFF`

The petrol and neutral palette retains a calm visual relationship with the
municipality's public website, but the logo itself is original to RötgesPortal.
The colors do not imply endorsement or official status.

## Independence

The official coat of arms of Rötgesbüttel is intentionally not included while
RötgesPortal is operated as an independent information service. Every public
surface must continue to state that the portal is independent and link to the
official municipal website. Any later adoption by the municipality requires an
explicit operating and brand decision rather than an implicit visual change.
