# Municipal visual reference

The portal UI takes its core visual cues from the public website of the
municipality of Rötgesbüttel:

- primary blue: `#006080`
- neutral background: `#EAEAEA`
- neutral text: `#444444`
- white surface: `#FFFFFF`
- municipal coat-of-arms image:
  `https://www.roetgesbuettel.de/media/custom/3985_2_1_k.PNG?1708350720`

Source: [Municipality of Rötgesbüttel](https://www.roetgesbuettel.de/), accessed
2026-08-03.

The coat of arms is stored locally so the portal does not disclose visits to the
municipal server and does not depend on an external image request. The portal is
currently an independent information service. Visual alignment and use of the
coat of arms must not be presented as municipal endorsement. Written permission
and an agreed brand policy should be confirmed before permanent production use
or a move to an official municipal domain.

The municipal website's legal notice remains the authoritative source for its
own content and rights. This repository records provenance; it does not grant a
license to third parties.
